The verdict

The closer booking system is correctly scoped — every booking on a closer calendar correctly fires intake, show-rate texts, no-show/cancel sync and deal-of-the-day tasks. The problem is five automations with no calendar filter that catch every booking in the account. Those are the live, current version of the "no calendar filter" leaks — and they're still open.
