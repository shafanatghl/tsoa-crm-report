The one thing to know

What's working

People are finding you and buying. Stripe took $174,204 in 30 days. Your closers booked 107 calls and closed $339,500. The top of the funnel is healthy.

What's broken

The follow-up is broken. 106 holes let leads fall out. 80 automations are built but switched off. Emails only reach 24–60% of inboxes. And several booking calendars send the customer no confirmation at all — they book, then hear nothing.

The 5 biggest holes right now

View all 106 →
P1Booking calendars send the customer no confirmation
The 6 "Follow-Up Call" calendars (Lauren, Oksana, Nick, Cassi, Catherine, Meenasha) and a few webinar calendars have the "Contact" box unchecked. A lead books, then gets nothing — no link, no reminder email.
P1Messages ask for "last year's federal tax"
Across 7 calendars, the confirmation SMS, closer emails and Reverse Audit emails ask leads to bring their last year's return or tax paid — the one thing they should not be asked.
P1Leads get lost between two automations
Two workflows ("PG" and "SX") quietly remove leads from other follow-ups the same second they start. Confirmed in the execution logs: leads vanish before ever getting an email.
P1Wojo leads get nothing (127 people)
Every Wojo webinar opt-in (127 matched) is meant to get a follow-up sequence, but all 4 Wojo workflows are switched off since Sep 8.
P1Emails don't arrive — or never get clicked
Delivery runs 24–60%. Five main router emails have 0% clicks out of ~12,000 sends, partly because click-tracking is switched off and date/time fields print blank.
