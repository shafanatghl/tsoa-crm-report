The problems

issueDiscovery Call has no "Qualified" branch
Every webhook lead — qualified or not — gets a Closer card at $20,000. Inflates Call Booked (71 cards) and the $2.23M open value. (Leak 43.)
issueJUAN: 5 SMS disabled, emails 0% delivered
All 5 SMS steps are off; Emails 1–7 show 0% delivered. Emails use {{appointment.*}} fields in a tag-triggered flow → dates print blank. (Leak 46.)
issueW-2 branch doesn't stop disqualified leads
The "Qualified? No" branch has an empty condition and rejoins the reminder path — W-2 registrants still get the full reminder sequence.
issueFunnel routes into other funnels; DQ URL is a 404
/act opt-in → /act-workbook (Opt In Lisette funnel); W-2 → /thankyou-disqualified (returns 404).
issuetsoa-intake-ready triggers nothing
The only workflow that triggers on that tag (WF01) is Draft. So the tag applied by the Discovery workflow leads nowhere.
copy"Federal taxes paid last year" on the LGG qualify page
LGG's external qualify page asks for federal tax paid + last three years of returns — outside GHL, but flagged.
