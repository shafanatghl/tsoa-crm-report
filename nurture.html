Where it breaks (confirmed in the logs)

PG deletes leads from the gate

When a Closer card moves to Nurture/Closed Lost, two robots fire at once: WF29 says "start the 21-day nurture", and PG says "this lead is pipeline-only, remove from 48 workflows" — including the nurture. PG wins the race. Lost closer leads never enter the nurture.

The stranded lock tag

Intake Gate locks each lead with tag nurture21-gating. If another robot (BR01, SP01, SX, PG) removes the lead mid-gate, the tag stays forever. Every future "wake" hits "Already gating → end". The lead can never enter the nurture again.

The SX problem (confirmed)

SX fires on any booking or any Brooke's/Closer card move — and removes the contact from BR01 and NURTURE 21. BR01's step 3 creates a Brooke's Pipeline card, which fires SX, which removes the contact from BR01 two seconds later. Result: everyone who entered BR01 after Oct 16 never got SMS 1 or SMS 2.
