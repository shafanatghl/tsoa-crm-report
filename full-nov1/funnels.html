The pattern

A tiny number of high-ticket pages bring almost all the money. Week 1 DFY: 134 views → 7 orders → $65,983 (that's $493 per visitor). Meanwhile the replay pages convert under 1% while the DFY pages do the heavy lifting — so the replay CTA should point at DFY.
