Why Ghada Lutfi got nothing

The exact case: Ghada Lutfi booked "Follow-Up Call with Lauren Bracken" for Nov 4. Her appointment was created and confirmed — but the calendar has the "Contact" box unchecked on every confirmation and reminder. So the closer got the notification, and Ghada got zero emails, zero texts. Nothing was ever sent to her.

The two fixes

1 · Tick "Contact"

On the 6 Follow-Up calendars and the Minimal/Webinar calendars, go to Advanced → Notifications and tick Contact on Booked (Confirmed) Email & SMS and on Reminder. Then set the reminder to 1 hour + 10 minutes before, like the Enrollment Advisory calendars.

2 · Fix the wording

On the closer calendars, replace the "Bring 3 rough numbers … last year's federal tax" SMS with the wording Oksana already uses: "Bring 2 rough numbers: last year's profit and this year's projected profit. Ballparks are fine."
