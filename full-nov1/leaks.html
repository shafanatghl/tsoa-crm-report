What the holes are, in plain words

The wrong question

Messages keep asking leads for "last year's tax return" and "federal tax paid" — the exact thing they shouldn't be asked. It's in 7 calendars, the confirmation pages, the Reverse Audit emails and the opt-in forms.

Bookings that go silent

Several calendars have the "Contact" box unchecked, so the customer books and gets no confirmation, no link, no reminder. Exactly what happened with Ghada Lutfi.

Robots fighting robots

Two automations (SX and PG) quietly delete leads from other follow-ups the same second they start. Leads vanish before they get one email.

Built but switched off

80 workflows are in Draft — fully built, just not running. Wojo leads (127 people) and closer cart reminders are the casualties.

Emails that don't land

Delivery is 24–60%. Five router emails have 0 clicks out of ~12,000 sends, partly because click-tracking is off and dates print blank.

Counts that lie

A ClickFunnels webhook fires 2,440 times with the same contact and blank emails, inflating your numbers and double-sending.
